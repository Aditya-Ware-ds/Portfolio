Research

Frequency vs. Difficulty: Limits of Classifier-Side Objectives for Long-Tailed Linear Probing

Completed · Sept.-2026

A controlled study of eight long-tailed losses for linear probing on Places-LT, with backbone, optimizer, and budget fixed so any gap traces to the objective alone. Shows the strongest losses sit within seed noise of one another, and that static frequency corrections structurally miss a set of “common-but-hard” classes that a frequency-blind difficulty signal partly recovers — at a measured cost to rare-but-easy classes.

Student–Teacher Architecture for Branch Staleness in Multi-Agent Autoresearch

Working paper · 2026

A trajectory-aware teacher–student framework for asynchronous multi-agent autoresearch. It scores each branch’s potential and lets a single discovery leak between promising branches without synchronizing full agent state — cutting branch staleness while keeping exploration independent.
